How It Boards

REPO IS THE DATABASE · ROBOT IS THE PUBLISHER
  • 1 — Connect once. Classic token, public_repo ticked, paste it in Settings. It stays in your browser. Then FILE FROM HERE sends tickets straight from this page — GitHub never opens.
  • 2 — Robot check. The publisher robot reads your ticket within about 5 minutes. Its reply (app ID, or what to fix) appears right under your filing in TRACK — no need to open GitHub.
  • 3 — Live. Good tickets board automatically — no approval taps needed. Your private app ID lands in your email (GitHub notifies you on the ticket reply) — save it, it never appears on any page, and it's your key for future edits.
  • 4 — Control. Bad apps can be removed with a Delete filing. If spam ever appears, the owner can switch on approvals with one word in data/publishers.json ("auto" → "approve").
